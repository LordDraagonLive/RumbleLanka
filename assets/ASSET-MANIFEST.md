# VS-01 placeholders

All runtime fighter/stage art is original geometric drawing in GDScript. `icon.svg` is an original geometric placeholder. `audio/placeholders/silence.tres` is eight samples of unsigned 8-bit digital silence at 8 kHz, used for every placeholder event with visible captions. No real voice, third-party artwork, music, or photograph is loaded or exported. The reference images supplied in the spec pack remain excluded by the Windows export filter. Replace presentation assets independently of combat.
